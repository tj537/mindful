# SWOT analysis

Strengths and weaknesses are internal; opportunities and threats come from outside.

## Strengths

- What do we do well?
- What unique resources do we have?

## Weaknesses

- Where can we improve?
- What do others do better?

## Opportunities

- Which trends can we use?
- Where are gaps in the market?

## Threats

- What are competitors doing?
- Which obstacles are coming up?
